Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to Flocash. This privacy statement provides information about the personal information that Flocash collects, and the ways in which Flocash uses that personal information.

Overview

1. Personal information collection

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Information about your use of our services;
  • Information that you provide for the purpose of registering for Flocash services;
  • Information about transactions carried out over Flocash services;

2. Using personal information

Flocash may use your personal information to:

  • Administer Flocash services;
  • Personalize the Flocash service for you;
  • Enable your access to and use of the Flocash services;
  • Publish information about you on the Flocash services;
  • Send you statements and invoices;
  • Collect payments from you; and
  • Send you marketing communications.

Where Flocash discloses your personal information to its agents or sub-contractors for these purposes, the agent or sub-contractor in question will be obligated to use that personal information in accordance with the terms of this privacy statement.

In addition to the disclosures reasonably necessary for the purposes identified elsewhere above, Flocash may disclose your personal information to the extent that it is required to do so by law, in connection with any legal proceedings or prospective legal proceedings, and in order to establish, exercise or defend its legal rights.

3. Securing your data

Flocash will take reasonable technical and organisational precautions to prevent the loss, misuse or alteration of your personal information. Flocash will store all the personal information you provide on its secure servers. Information relating to electronic transactions entered into via our services will be protected by encryption technology.

4. Cross-border data transfers

Information that Flocash collects may be stored and processed in and transferred between any of the countries in which Flocash operates to enable the use of the information in accordance with this privacy policy.

You agree to such cross-border transfers of personal information.

5. Updating this statement

Flocash may update this privacy policy by posting a new version on our services.

You should check this page occasionally to ensure you are familiar with any changes.

6. Other websites

Our services contain links to other websites.

Flocash is not responsible for the privacy policies or practices of any third party.

7. General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)

We are a Data Controller of your information.

Flocash legal basis for collecting and using the personal information described in this Privacy Policy depends on the Personal Information we collect and the specific context in which we collect the information:

  • Flocash needs to perform a contract with you
  • You have given Flocash permission to do so
  • Processing your personal information is in Flocash legitimate interests
  • Flocash needs to comply with the law

Flocash will retain your personal information only for as long as is necessary for the purposes set out in this Privacy Policy. We will retain and use your information to the extent necessary to comply with our legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our policies.

If you are a resident of the European Economic Area (EEA), you have certain data protection rights. If you wish to be informed what Personal Information, we hold about you and if you want it to be removed from our systems, please contact us.

In certain circumstances, you have the following data protection rights:

  • The right to access, update or to delete the information we have on you.
  • The right of rectification.
  • The right to object.
  • The right of restriction.
  • The right to data portability
  • The right to withdraw consent

8. Cookies

Flocash uses 'cookies'. These cookies are used to store information including visitors' preferences, and the pages on the website that the visitor accessed or visited. The information is used to optimize the users' experience by customizing our web page content based on visitors' browser type and/or other information.

9. Consent

By using our website, you hereby consent to our Privacy Policy and agree to its Terms and Conditions.

10. Contact Flocash

If you have any questions about this privacy policy or Flocash's treatment of your personal information, email to info@flocash.com
