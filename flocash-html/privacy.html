Overview

Information We Collect How We Use Your Data Data Sharing Data Retention Your Rights Security Changes to This Policy Contact Us

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Account Details: Name, email address, and login credentials
  • Usage Data: Interactions with our platform, preferences, and activity history
  • Integration Data: Information shared through connected third-party tools (e.g., Notion, Slack)
  • Device Information: Browser type, operating system, IP address

2. How We Use Your Data

We use collected data to:

  • Operate and improve our platform
  • Personalize your experience
  • Communicate updates, offers, and support
  • Maintain security and prevent abuse

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. We may share limited data only with:

  • Trusted third-party service providers (e.g., analytics, hosting)
  • Legal authorities when required by law

4. Data Retention

We retain your data only as long as necessary to provide our services and comply with legal obligations.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access or request deletion of your data
  • Update or correct your information
  • Opt out of promotional communications

6. Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data. However, no system is completely secure.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Significant changes will be communicated via email or platform notifications.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions, please contact us at: support@yourcompany.com
