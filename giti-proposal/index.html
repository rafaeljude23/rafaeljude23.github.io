01 · Challenge

The Gap Between
Visitors & Conversions

Giti's existing Tire Finder is form-based — users need to already know their vehicle specs. Most don't. This creates friction that turns potential buyers into bounced sessions.

🔍

Spec Confusion

Most drivers don't know their tire size, aspect ratio, or speed rating. Drop-down forms intimidate rather than guide.

🌍

Global Audiences

Giti serves markets across Asia, Europe, the Americas and the Middle East — each with different driving needs and climates.

📊

Product Depth

With dozens of SKUs across GitiComfort, GitiSport, Giti4×4 and more, selecting the right tire demands expert knowledge.

02 · Proposed Solution

AI-Guided Tire
Selection Flow

A conversational AI assistant embedded on giti.com that asks simple questions, understands the driver's context, and delivers a confident product recommendation with clear reasoning.

1

Entry Point

User lands on giti.com. A persistent "Find My Tire" widget opens via floating button or dedicated page — no forms, just a conversation starter.

Sample Prompt

"Hi! Let's find the perfect Giti tire for you. What kind of vehicle do you drive — a city car, SUV, or something sporty?"

2

Smart Intake

The AI collects driving profile through natural language: vehicle type, region & climate, primary use (highway, city, off-road), and priority (comfort, performance, longevity, wet-grip).

Quick Picks Available

Visual chip buttons for fast answers: "City commuter · Daily driver · Weekend adventure" — so users aren't forced to type.

3

Intelligent Matching

The AI cross-references inputs against Giti's full product catalog — series, rim sizes, speed ratings, EV readiness, and AdvanZtech technology tiers.

Logic Layer

RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) over Giti product data ensures recommendations stay accurate and up-to-date with zero hallucination.

4

Recommendation & Explanation

Top 1–3 tires are presented as rich cards with performance bars, key benefits, and a plain-English reason why each was chosen for this specific driver.

Trust Builder

Each card links directly to the full product page, the Tire Performance Simulator, and a dealer locator — reducing drop-off to zero.

5

Handoff & Conversion

Users can save results, share the recommendation link, or instantly connect with a regional dealer. Session data feeds Giti's analytics and CRM.

Outcome

Every conversation captures intent signals — vehicle, region, use case — creating a rich first-party data asset for Giti's marketing team.

04 · Key Features

Built For
Every Driver

01

Conversational AI Core

Powered by a large language model with retrieval-augmented generation over Giti's product catalog. No hallucinations — every recommendation is grounded in real SKU data. The conversation adapts fluidly: a user can correct themselves, ask follow-ups, or compare two options at any time.

Claude API / LLM
02

Multilingual Support

Giti operates across 6 continents and dozens of regional sites. The advisor automatically detects user language or site locale — serving the same experience in English, Mandarin, Bahasa, Thai, Spanish, German, and Arabic with zero latency penalty.

i18n · 12+ Languages
03

EV & AdvanZtech Aware

As Giti expands its EV-ready lineup, the advisor flags AdvanZtech 2.0 compatible tires when users identify an electric or hybrid vehicle. This proactively educates on Giti's technology edge — turning a feature into a sales moment.

Product Intelligence
04

Climate & Region Logic

Driving conditions vary wildly across Giti's global markets. The advisor factors in local climate (wet tropical, arid desert, snowy winter) automatically from region or user input — recommending winter variants like the WT80 where appropriate.

Geo-Contextual AI
05

Analytics & CRM Pipeline

Every advisor session generates structured intent data: vehicle type, region, use case, priority attributes, and recommended SKU. This feeds Giti's marketing analytics and can sync to CRM systems for dealer follow-up — transforming anonymous traffic into qualified leads.

Data · First-Party
06

Seamless Ecosystem Integration

The advisor lives natively within giti.com, sharing its visual language and dark design system. Recommended tires link directly to product pages, the Tire Performance Simulator, and the dealer locator — creating a frictionless path to purchase with zero dead ends.

UX · Integration
05 · Project Scope

Delivery Plan

A three-phase rollout, from foundational build to global scale.

Deliverable Phase Timeline Description
Product Knowledge Base Phase 1 Wk 1–2 Ingest and structure Giti's full tire catalog into a vector database for retrieval-augmented generation. Establish product metadata schema.
AI Advisor Engine Phase 1 Wk 2–5 Build and test the conversational AI system prompt, intake logic, matching algorithm, and recommendation output structure.
UI / UX Design Phase 1 Wk 3–5 Design the widget and full-page advisor interface aligned with Giti's design system. Includes chat UI, result cards, and quick-pick chips.
giti.com Integration Phase 2 Wk 6–7 Embed the advisor as a floating widget and dedicated page on giti.com global. Connect to Tire Performance Simulator and dealer locator.
Multilingual Launch Phase 2 Wk 7–8 Localize advisor conversations and UI for priority regional sites: EU, LAM, Middle East, Asia. Climate and product filters per region.
Analytics Dashboard Phase 2 Wk 8 Real-time dashboard for Giti's marketing team: conversation volume, top recommended SKUs, drop-off points, and regional interest heatmaps.
Fleet / B2B Variant Phase 3 Wk 9–10 Separate advisor flow for fleet.giti.com targeting truck and bus fleet managers, with load index, axle position, and mileage guarantee logic.
CRM & Lead Export Phase 3 Wk 10 Optional CRM sync (Salesforce / HubSpot) for qualified advisor sessions. Auto-generated dealer referral emails based on user region and selected SKU.
